3. Fix it - grams to target

Targets are mid-range published values (Ca 420 ppm, Alk 8.5 dKH, Mg 1300 ppm). Splits follow commonly published safe daily-change guidance (Ca 50 ppm, Alk 1.4 dKH, Mg 100 ppm per day). Anything already at or above target is left alone - lowering is a water-change job.

4. Daily two-part (keep-up dosing)

Calcification consumes calcium with alkalinity in a fixed ratio (20 ppm Ca per meq/L, about 7.1 ppm per dKH), so the calcium part follows from your alkalinity consumption.

6. Limewater (kalkwasser)

Model: ideal-solution stoichiometry from published molecular weights; 1 meq/L = 2.8 dKH by convention; saturated limewater taken as ~40.8 meq/L and ~804 ppm Ca at 25 C (published). Real salt purity, hydration state, test-kit error (often +/-5-10%) and tank pH shift outcomes. Verify with a test kit and raise slowly.
