ReefDose

Your corals are drinking calcium and alkalinity every day, and the answer is never "a scoop of whatever". ReefDose turns three test-kit numbers into exact grams: how much two-part to reach your targets, how much to dose every day to keep up, and what your next water change will actually do.

Dose my tank
  • Fix it - grams of calcium chloride, baking soda or soda ash, and magnesium chloride to reach published target ranges, split into safe daily steps.
  • Daily two-part - enter your measured alkalinity consumption and get both halves of the day's dose, calcium included, from calcification stoichiometry.
  • Water-change truth - a change only moves numbers toward your salt mix. See the real result before you mix a bucket.
  • Limewater (kalkwasser) - what liters per day of saturated limewater actually adds, in dKH and ppm.
  • Runs entirely in your browser. Ideal-solution chemistry with every assumption labeled.

Target ranges (Ca 380-450 ppm, Alk 7-11 dKH, Mg 1250-1350 ppm) and safe daily-change guidance are commonly published reef-keeping values. Dosing amounts are ideal stoichiometry from published molecular weights; salt purity, hydration state and test-kit error still apply. Raise slowly and re-test.
